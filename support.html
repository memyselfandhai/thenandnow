Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email memyselfandhai@gmail.com, or use Settings → Send Feedback in the app, which adds your app and iOS version automatically.

Common questions

The app shows no photos, or only some

Open the iPhone Settings app → Apps → Then & Now → Photos and choose Full Access. With "Limited Access", the app can only see the photos you selected.

Some of my older photos are missing

If your photos are in iCloud, make sure iCloud Photos is turned on for this iPhone (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos). Then & Now shows everything in your iPhone's Photos library, including iCloud photos. To include iCloud Shared Albums, turn on Settings → Include Shared Albums in the app.

How do I remove ads?

In the app, open Settings → Ads and choose Remove Ads Forever (one-time purchase) or Ad-Free Monthly (subscription). Your first 30 days are ad-free either way.

I paid to remove ads, but I see ads again

After reinstalling or on a new device, open Settings → Ads → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. If you had the monthly subscription, check it's still active in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

In the app, open Settings → Ads → Manage Subscription, or go to your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Then & Now. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Ads stay off until the end of the month you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted and recover it. Items stay there for about 30 days.

Captions don't show

Turn on Settings → Show Caption in the app. Showing captions requires iOS 27 or later.
