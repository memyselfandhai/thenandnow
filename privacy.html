Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device. We never see, upload or store them.
  • There are no accounts, and we don't run our own analytics or sell your information.
  • After a free first week, the app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Removing ads stops this.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

This policy explains what information the Then & Now iPhone app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by its independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions: memyselfandhai@gmail.com.

1. Your photos and videos

With your permission, the app reads your photo library on your device to show the photos and videos taken on a chosen date. This includes each item's date, and — if you turn the settings on — its location and caption. All of this happens on your device.

  • We never upload, copy to our servers, or have access to your photos, videos or their details.
  • The app only changes your library when you ask it to: favoriting, deleting, hiding, duplicating, adding to an album, or adjusting an item's date, time or location. Deleted items go to your Recently Deleted album, as in the Photos app.
  • If iCloud Photos is turned on, Apple's Photos framework may download originals from iCloud when you open them. That transfer is between your device and Apple.
  • You can change or remove photo access at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Apps → Then & Now → Photos.

2. Place names and location search

  • Show Location (off by default): to show a place name such as "Austin, Texas", the app sends the photo's stored coordinates to Apple's location service, which returns the name.
  • Adjust Info: when you search for a new location, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places.

These requests go to Apple, not to us, and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not use your device's current location.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Your settings (sort order, display options, and so on) are saved on your device.
  • The start of your ad-free first week is saved in your device's Keychain, which iOS keeps even if the app is deleted, so reinstalling doesn't restart the free week. The app also checks Apple's record of when your Apple Account first downloaded the app. Neither is sent to us.
  • Whether you've removed ads is saved on your device so ads stay off when you're offline.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free with ads. Ads are not shown during your first week, and never after you remove them. When ads are on, the app uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show an occasional full-page ad.

To show, measure and protect against fraud in ads, Google may collect information such as your IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and operating system details, app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not give Google your device's advertising identifier.

Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy — see also How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Removing ads (one-time purchase or monthly subscription) stops ads and the related data collection.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

"Remove Ads Forever" and "Ad-Free Monthly" are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple of which purchase is active, so it can turn ads off. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Feedback emails

If you choose Send Feedback, your email app opens a message to us. It includes the app version, iOS version and device model, which you can see and edit before sending. If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, keep it only as long as needed for that, and never share or sell it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or collect your name, email or phone number (unless you email us).
  • We don't use our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it except as described above.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to certain uses. Because we hold no personal information apart from emails you send us, most requests can be handled by emailing memyselfandhai@gmail.com. For data collected by Google or Apple, use their privacy tools linked above.

10. Security

Your photos are protected by iOS and never pass through our systems. Feedback emails are kept in a secured email account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: memyselfandhai@gmail.com
